## 1. Introduction
Kids P2P Marketplace ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Kids P2P Marketplace mobile
application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect,
how we use it, and the choices you have. The Service is directed to children and
families; we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the
United States and similar laws (e.g., GDPR-K in the EU/UK where applicable).

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account data**: email address, password (hashed), display name.
- **Optional verification**: phone number (for trust gating only; never required for login).
- **Profile data**: profile photo (optional), date of birth (for age gating), ZIP code.
- **Listings**: item title, description, price, photos.
- **Messages**: in-app messages between buyers and sellers.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device info**: OS version, device model, app version.
- **Usage data**: screens visited, actions taken (anonymized).
- **Approximate location**: ZIP-based; we do NOT collect precise GPS coordinates.
- **Crash diagnostics**: stack traces and device metadata (no PII) via Sentry.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Stripe**: payout account verification data when sellers connect a bank account.

## 3. How We Use Information
- Operate the marketplace (matchmaking, listings, transactions).
- Process payments and payouts via Stripe.
- Send transactional notifications (purchase confirmations, message alerts).
- Detect fraud, abuse, and policy violations.
- Improve the Service (aggregated, anonymized analytics only).
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do NOT use personal data for advertising or behavioral profiling.

## 4. Children Under 13 (COPPA)
- A parent or legal guardian must provide verifiable consent before a child under
  13 can register or use the Service.
- We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the Service for children.
- We do not use children's personal information for targeted advertising.
- Parents may review, update, or request deletion of their child's data at any
  time by contacting [privacy@kidsp2p.example.com].

## 5. Information Sharing
We share information only with:
- **Stripe** — payment processing ([https://stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy))
- **Firebase Analytics** — anonymized usage analytics ([https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy))
- **Sentry** — crash diagnostics ([https://sentry.io/privacy/](https://sentry.io/privacy/))
- **Law enforcement** — only when legally compelled.

We do NOT sell or rent personal information.

## 6. Data Security
- All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit
  using TLS 1.2 or higher.
- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plaintext.
- Database access is restricted by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices
You can:
- View and edit your profile information at any time.
- Request a copy of your data.
- Request deletion of your account and associated data (Profile → Delete Account).
- Opt out of optional notifications.

## 8. Data Retention
- Active account data is retained while your account is active.
- Transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years for tax/legal compliance.
- Deleted-account data is purged within 30 days of deletion request (except where
  legally required to retain).

## 9. Third-Party Services
The Service uses third-party services listed in Section 5. Each operates under its
own privacy policy. We do not control how those services handle data they collect
directly from you.

## 10. International Users
Data is processed and stored in the United States. By using the Service from
outside the US you consent to such transfer.

## 11. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated via in-app
notification and an updated Effective Date. Continued use after changes constitutes
acceptance.
